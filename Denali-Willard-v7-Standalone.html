Project terms, scope & privacy information +

Nonbinding planning estimate. Prices shown here are for budgeting purposes and do not create a contract or purchase obligation. Final scope, timeline, deliverables and pricing are set in a written agreement. Scope changes may require a revised quote.

Excluded unless stated. Printing, postage, advertising, statistically representative household sampling, and major additional research activities are not included. The standard citizen survey represents voluntary community input rather than a probability sample.

Privacy & public records. Denali will agree survey data handling, reporting thresholds, record retention and access procedures with the City in advance, subject to applicable Missouri public-records obligations. Employee stay interviews are confidential, not anonymous.

Multiyear agreements. Any three-year pricing requires a written agreement subject to City procurement, funding and annual appropriation provisions. Only the annual employee survey is included in quoted renewals.

Website information. This page does not collect survey responses and does not submit selections unless a proposal request endpoint is explicitly configured. Share links contain selections but exclude a private target budget.
